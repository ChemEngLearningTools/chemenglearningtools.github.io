What is ChemEngBench?

ChemEngBench is an educational and research application organised around a structured chemical-engineering question benchmark. The public interface provides access to benchmark questions, worked material and evaluation context.

It retains separate benchmark and validation resources so that the research basis can be examined instead of treating benchmark scores as unexplained outcomes.

Useful for

  • Exploring chemical-engineering benchmark questions
  • Reviewing worked solutions and methodology
  • Understanding dataset construction and evaluation
  • Examining human validation and research findings
