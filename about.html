About the project

Chemical Engineering Learning Tools

A growing collection of free educational applications designed to make chemical-engineering concepts easier to explore, visualise and teach.

Why these tools exist

Many chemical-engineering topics become clearer when equations, diagrams and dynamic behaviour can be explored together. These tools are designed around that idea.

The applications emphasise transparent engineering models, progressive explanations and interactive examples rather than black-box calculation.

Current collection

  • ProcessControlPot: process dynamics and process control.
  • ReactionPot: chemical reaction engineering and reactor simulation.
  • ChemEngDiagramPot: engineering diagrams and teaching figures.

Educational scope

The tools are intended for students, educators and individual learners. They support learning and teaching; they are not substitutes for professional design, industrial validation or process-safety procedures.
