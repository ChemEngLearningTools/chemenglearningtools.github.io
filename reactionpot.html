What is ReactionPot?

ReactionPot is a free browser-based educational application for chemical reaction engineering. It combines fundamentals with interactive reactor models and numerical simulation.

The aim is to make the structure of the model explicit: stoichiometry, reaction rates, balances, thermal effects and numerical integration are treated as connected engineering ideas rather than hidden calculations.

Useful for

  • Reaction-engineering fundamentals
  • Batch and fed-batch reactor teaching
  • Non-isothermal reactor behaviour
  • Numerical ODE solution
  • Exploring conversion, yield and selectivity

Topics currently covered

StoichiometryStoichiometric coefficients and vectors, stoichiometric matrices, species rates and the distinction between stoichiometry and kinetics.
Conversion, yield & selectivityCore measures used to interpret reactor performance and competing reaction pathways.
Batch reactor simulationIsothermal and non-isothermal models, including worked reaction systems and dynamic response.
Fed-batch operationDynamic feed strategies and the engineering trade-offs that distinguish fed-batch operation from simple batch operation.
Thermal behaviour & jacket dynamicsHeat effects, reactor temperature, jacket behaviour and feedback-control examples.
Numerical differential equationsWhy numerical integration provides a general framework, including practical treatment of discontinuities and changing operating conditions.
Educational use: ReactionPot is intended for teaching and learning chemical reaction engineering. It is not a substitute for validated industrial reactor design or process-safety analysis.
