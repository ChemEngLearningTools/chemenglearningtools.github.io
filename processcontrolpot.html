What is ProcessControlPot?

ProcessControlPot is a free browser-based chemical engineering learning tool designed to connect process-control theory with dynamic simulation and engineering interpretation.

It develops ideas progressively: from process dynamics and transfer functions, through feedback and block diagrams, to controller tuning and feedforward control.

Useful for

  • Undergraduate process-control teaching
  • Independent study and revision
  • Visualising dynamic response
  • Comparing controller-design methods
  • Connecting equations with time-domain behaviour

Topics currently covered

The public app currently includes the following established teaching areas.

Process dynamicsFundamental modelling, transfer functions, first-order and FOPDT behaviour, model identification and dynamic-response simulation.
Feedback & block diagramsClosed-loop structure, governing relationships, set-point and disturbance response, and block-diagram interpretation.
Controller tuningTuning fundamentals, Ziegler–Nichols, Direct Synthesis and SIMC, with simulation-based comparison of response and disturbance rejection.
Feedforward controlFeedforward fundamentals, model-based compensation, realizability, model mismatch and combined feedforward-plus-feedback simulation.
Educational use: ProcessControlPot is designed for learning, research and teaching support. It is not a substitute for industrial control-system design, commissioning or safety analysis.
