Teaching philosophy

Engineering first, software second

The tools are designed to make underlying engineering relationships visible. Simulations, diagrams and numerical results are presented as aids to understanding rather than as black-box answers.

Designed for learning

  • Clear explanations alongside calculations and simulations.
  • Interactive examples that connect equations to engineering behaviour.
  • Useful for lectures, tutorials, independent study and revision.
  • Free to access in a web browser.
