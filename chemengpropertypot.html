What is ChemEngPropertyPot?

ChemEngPropertyPot is a browser-based property explorer and reference application. It makes the selected data sources, numerical methods and assumptions visible alongside calculated results.

Its workspaces are deliberately limited in scope: for example, mixture-property calculations do not replace rigorous phase-equilibrium analysis, and the solubility workspace uses user-selected Henry’s law data.

Useful for

  • Checking individual species properties and method provenance
  • Working with IAPWS water and steam tables
  • Exploring engineering materials and utility fluids
  • Interpreting mixture-property methods and limitations
  • Applying Henry’s law to dissolved gases in water
